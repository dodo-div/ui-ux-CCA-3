Conclusion

Feedback from the five participants (A to E) shows that the redesigned FamApp was preferred over the original FamPay by every one of them.
They described the original as busy, cramped and tiring, with too many banners, offers and icons competing for attention, which made even simple tasks like checking the balance take extra effort.
The redesign felt calmer, cleaner and more student-friendly.

The most appreciated features were the large balance on the dark card, the orange Scan & Pay button that clearly signals the main action, and the quick-action row for daily tasks.
Participants also valued the “This Month” spending breakdown, which showed where money goes at a glance, and the Keeper savings goal, which they found motivating.
The friendly greeting, clear section headings, logical top-to-bottom order and the tidy transaction list with status labels also made the app easy to understand.

Some problems came up repeatedly. The duplicate “Pay” and “Scan & Pay” buttons caused confusion.
Small, light text, including bottom navigation labels, secondary text and the “View all” link, was hard to read, and the quick-action icons were close enough to cause a mis-tap.
The Failed and Pending statuses were too subtle and gave no reason or next step such as Retry. Unclear names like “Keeper”, “Wallet” versus “Home”, and the “CARD” tag left users guessing.
Participants also noted the long scrolling page, the lack of search, the missing payment confirmation step and the absence of error and empty states.

Overall, the redesign clearly improves clarity, speed and user confidence. Merging the Pay buttons, enlarging small text,
using stronger colours for warnings, making failed payments actionable and clarifying labels would make it a much more complete product.
